**Task 3: Extract Business Context & Financial Data**



**Task 3 Objective (Why this section exists):**

Use this section to support NAICS classification and granular segment/product mapping.
*   PRIORITIZE **explicit qualitative descriptions** of what the company does in each segment/product (activities, offerings, end-markets) — NOT revenue language in the description fields.
*   Segment and product description entries are REQUIRED **only for items that appear in the disclosed segment/product revenue or mix breakdown**. Do not invent segments/products that have no breakdown row.
*   Revenue numbers belong **only** in the Financial Data Table (not inside Segment/Product description text).
*   Capture exact language and page numbers for qualitative descriptions.
*   If product-level disclosure is more granular than segment-level, prioritize product-level details for mapping; retain both when both exist.

**Mandatory sequence for Task 3 (must follow in order):**

1.  **Capture revenue rows (allowed financials only):**
    *   Consolidated Revenue from the consolidated income / P&L statement.
    *   Standalone Revenue if disclosed.
    *   Segment Revenue - [name] for each segment in the breakdown.
    *   Product Revenue - [name] for each product/category in a product-wise breakdown (if any).
    *   **FORBIDDEN financials:** dividends, PAT, EBITDA, EBIT, assets, liabilities, cash flow, EPS, borrowings, share capital, tax, provisions, or any other statement line items not listed above.

2.  **Segment qualitative descriptions (breakdown segments only):**
    *   For each segment that appears in the segment revenue/mix breakdown, extract an explicit description of **what that segment is / what the company does in it**.
    *   No revenue, percentages, or financial figures in the description.

3.  **Product/service qualitative descriptions (breakdown products only):**
    *   For each product/category/service in a product-wise breakdown, extract an explicit description of **what it is / what the company offers**.
    *   No revenue or percentage metrics in the description.

4.  **Revenue consistency check:**
    *   Compare total segment and/or product breakdown against consolidated revenue.
    *   Mark "Matches Consolidated Revenue" or "Does Not Match Consolidated Revenue". Do not drop breakdowns if reconciliation fails.

5.  **Granularity decision:** "Segment-based" or "Product-based" (more granular available basis).

6.  **Organizational entities / subsidiaries:** Capture group entities (subsidiaries, JVs, associates, key reporting entities) and what they do if stated.

7.  **Related ESG reports / links / scope:** If the company mentions other ESG/sustainability/BRSR/CDP/TCFD reports, portals, or URLs, and any scope notes tied to them, extract those.
