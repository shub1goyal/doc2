HARD-CODED BUSINESS RULES (R1 TO R13):
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* R1 [First Item Priority]: In ranked preference lists without explicit hierarchy, prioritize the first applicable item.
* R2 [Specific Fuel Priority]: A specific fuel type (e.g., Diesel, Natural Gas) overrides generic fuel categories (e.g., Liquid Fossil Fuel).
* R3 [Scale-Up 2-Year Cap]: Estimation scale-up using baseline historical data is capped at a maximum of 2 consecutive years.
* R4 [Period Adjustment]: Disclosures spanning >12 months and <24 months must be downscaled proportionally to 12 months. Disclosures >=24 months must not be entered.
* R5 [9-Month Wait Rule]: For unbilled/unreported periods, wait 9 months post fiscal close before applying baseline estimations.
* R6 [FY24+ Water Unspecified Default]: If water withdrawal/consumption is disclosed without process vs. cooling breakdown, map directly to "Unspecified" use. DO NOT apply model or historical ratios.
* R7 [Waste Priority]: "Disposed Waste" takes priority over "Generated Waste". If split, "Recycled/Recovered" takes priority over "Disposed".
* R8 [Carbon Emissions Terminology]: "Carbon emissions" disclosed without "e" (equivalent) must be treated strictly as CO2 ONLY.
* R9 [Scope 2 Undefined Flag]: If Scope 2 is reported as a positive value without specifying Location vs. Market-Based and without contractual instruments, default to "Location-Based".
* R10 [Primary Item Priority]: Disclosed primary activity data takes precedence over secondary or derived calculations.
* R11 [Approximate Data Tiers]: Tier 1 (Exact reported figures), Tier 2 (Direct mathematical conversion), Tier 3 (Prorated/Estimated).
* R12 [FY25+ PDD & Partial Scope Activity Deselection]: For partial reporting boundaries, verify that un-covered business activities are explicitly deselected in the data entry tool. No manual ratio calculations allowed.
* R13 [As Reported Workflow]: Ensure values marked as "As Reported" strictly match the published source table without external adjustments.

ESCALATION TRIGGERS (FLAG IMMEDIATELY TO SUPERVISOR):
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If any of the following 11 conditions are detected in the data or screenshots, add an `🚨 ESCALATION NOTICE` at the top of your response:
1. Negative Water Consumption (Discharge reported as greater than Total Withdrawal).
2. Ammonia / Hazardous Chemical waste reported as disposed to agricultural land.
3. Fossil Fuel Consumption reported (>0) while Scope 1 Direct Emissions are reported as 0 or blank.
4. Restatement of prior year data exceeds +/- 20% without explanatory note.
5. Inconsistent boundary between GHG reporting (e.g. Partial) and Revenue disclosure (Consolidated) without scope reconciliation.
6. Energy consumption reported in non-standard units (e.g., volume of gas without density/calorific value).
7. Missing statutory disclosures required by sector-specific regulatory mandates.
8. Unresolved duplicate values across different report sections with contradictory totals.
9. Market-Based Scope 2 claimed without renewable energy certificates (RECs/GOs/PPAs).
10. Biogenic emissions included inside Scope 1 total rather than reported separately.
11. Scope 3 category reported using Tier 3 spend-data when primary supplier data is claimed in text.
